# Intercity traffic

Verified against game version 1.6.0f1.

**Read this with the decompile open.**
Without one you cannot check anything below.
`cs2-modding-setup` provisions it.

Through traffic is generated by spawner buildings at the map edge, routed spawner-to-spawner with a deliberately random pairing, and never touches the city's economy — its delivery trucks carry `DeliveryTruckFlags.DummyTraffic`.

## Generation

`TrafficSpawnerAISystem.Tick` computes `rate = TrafficSpawnerData.m_SpawnRate * 4.266667f`, draws uniformly in `[0.5 * rate, 1.5 * rate)`, rounds stochastically, and requests a vehicle when none is already outstanding — the rate field is prefab data on the spawner (`src/Game/Game.Simulation/TrafficSpawnerAISystem.cs`, `src/Game/Game.Prefabs/TrafficSpawnerData.cs`).
During the new-game warm-up (`m_Loading < 0.9` — a save load snaps the progress straight to 1, so only a new game runs this branch), a road spawner spawns two vehicles per request, an airplane spawner up to one and a train spawner none, each placed directly in the middle stretch of its own path when that path is long enough, with no spawn delay — which is how the road network is pre-populated; afterwards one vehicle spawns at the connection with up to 256 frames of random delay.

## Routing

`RandomTrafficDispatchSystem` builds the request with the neutral weights `(1, 1, 1, 1)` and `m_IgnoredRules = ForbidSlowTraffic | AvoidBicycles`, adds `PathMethod.MediumRoad` for the Small and Medium size classes and `CargoLoading` for delivery trucks, ignores `ForbidHeavyTraffic` for those same two classes, and swaps origin and destination on a coin flip (`src/Game/Game.Simulation/RandomTrafficDispatchSystem.cs`).
The candidate pairing is randomised before the search: `RoadPathfindSetup` offers every other spawner whose road or track types intersect the request's, each at a cost drawn uniformly from `[0, 10000)` (`src/Game/Game.Simulation/RoadPathfindSetup.cs`).
So which pair of connections a given vehicle links is substantially random, and building a shorter route between two connections shifts the distribution rather than redirecting the flow.

## The border feeds congestion back as time cost

`Game.Net.OutsideConnection` holds one field, `m_Delay`, and it is measured, not authored.
`OutsideConnectionDelaySystem` finds, on the connected edges, the road and bicycle lanes whose start joins the connection, and sums each one's entry dwell: the `CarCurrentLane.m_Duration` of every vehicle still on its entry stretch — `ResetSpeed` set, which marks a freshly spawned or connection-leaving car until it has travelled roughly ten metres — so the sum grows exactly when the entry is congested; the mean over those lanes goes into `m_Delay`, and a `Time` action sets the connection edge's time cost to `PathfindConnectionData.m_BorderCost.m_Value.x + m_Delay` (`src/Game/Game.Simulation/OutsideConnectionDelaySystem.cs`, `src/Game/Game.Simulation/CarNavigationSystem.cs`).
`PathUtils.GetSpecification` seeds a connection lane's time cost from `m_Delay` directly, so a queue at one border crossing raises that crossing's price for every agent choosing between crossings (`src/Game/Game.Pathfind/PathUtils.cs`).
The border costs themselves are prefab values on the pathfind prefab carrying `PathfindConnectionData`; the connection as an object, and what arrives through it, belong to `transportation-and-vehicles`.
